Spain University Outreach

Your contacts, three emails each: the first contact, a follow-up 2 working days later, and a last follow-up 3 working days after that. Weekends and Spanish public holidays don't count. The contact list lives in your OneDrive and is loaded after you sign in; everything else is read from your Outlook: an email is only counted once it appears in Sent Items, a reply is detected from your Inbox, and a conversation leaves this page once you have answered the reply.
